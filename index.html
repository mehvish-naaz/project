WINNE THE POOH

Winnie the Pooh is a beloved, honey-loving anthropomorphic teddy bear created by English author A. A. Milne in 1926.
He lives in the fictional Hundred Acre Wood alongside a cast of memorable animal friends and the human boy Christopher Robin.
His closest companions include the timid Piglet, the gloomy donkey Eeyore, and the highly energetic Tigger.
